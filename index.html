How sustainability is graded

This rubric is a quick comparison tool for students choosing lower-emission ways to get around.

Grade A

Very low direct emissions or very efficient shared travel. Typical examples: metro, tram, electrified rail, high-ridership regional rail, or public bike systems.

Grade B

Lower-emission shared travel, but not as efficient as top-grade electric rail or bike systems. Typical examples: city buses, mixed bus-and-rail systems, or airport buses with solid occupancy.

Grade C

Shared transport with higher per-person emissions or inconsistent occupancy. Typical examples: road-based airport coaches, shared shuttles, or mixed systems that rely heavily on combustion vehicles.

Grade D

Highest-emission common choice for the same trip. Typical examples: private taxis or solo ride-hail when good shared alternatives exist.
